About Pune

Pune, often called the "Oxford of the East," is a major metropolis in Maharashtra, India, located on the Deccan Plateau. With a population of over 7 million in its metropolitan region, Pune is the second largest city in Maharashtra after Mumbai. The city has a rich history dating back to the 8th century and was the seat of the Peshwas during the Maratha Empire. Pune is renowned for its educational institutions, vibrant culture, and as a major IT and automobile hub. The city is also known for its pleasant climate, green hills, and the confluence of the Mula and Mutha rivers. Pune has been ranked as one of the most liveable cities in India and is famous for its festivals, food, and cosmopolitan lifestyle. It is a center for classical music, theater, sports, and spiritualism, and is home to many historic landmarks such as Shaniwar Wada, Aga Khan Palace, and Pataleshwar Cave Temple.
