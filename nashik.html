About Nashik

Nashik, located on the banks of the Godavari River, is one of Maharashtra's most ancient and culturally significant cities. Known for hosting the Kumbh Mela every 12 years, Nashik is a major pilgrimage destination with temples like Trimbakeshwar and Panchavati. The city is also famous for its vineyards and wine tourism, earning it the title 'Wine Capital of India.' Nashik blends spiritual heritage with modern industry, scenic hills, and a pleasant climate, making it a unique destination for all types of travelers.

Quick Facts

  • Location: Maharashtra, India
  • Population: 2.1 million+
  • Languages: Marathi, Hindi, English
  • Best Time to Visit: October to March

Local Cuisine

Misal Pav

Spicy Maharashtrian curry served with bread, a Nashik breakfast staple. Try it at Sadhana Restaurant.

Sabudana Khichdi

Popular fasting food made from sago pearls, peanuts, and spices, available at many Nashik eateries.

Maharashtrian Thali

A platter of local delicacies including puran poli, bhakri, pithla, and more. Try at Panchavati Gaurav.
